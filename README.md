About the Game

Lost in Orbit is a 2D space platformer developed using the Godot Engine. The game takes place in the vastness of space, where players must navigate through low-gravity environments, using momentum and carefully timed movements to travel between floating debris, asteroids, and planets.

The objective is to make your way back to your damaged spaceship while collecting scattered spaceship components and Space Gem Shards needed to restore its power.

With an emphasis on movement, exploration, and platforming, Lost in Orbit challenges players to use the unusual physics of space to their advantage while facing the dangers hidden throughout the universe.

Story

Everything was going according to plan.

You are an astronaut assigned to repair a malfunctioning satellite. Your mission seems straightforward, and everything appears normal as you approach your destination.

Until a comet comes out of nowhere.

The comet tears through your rocket, completely destroys the satellite, and leaves you stranded in the endless void of space.

Your spaceship is damaged, its components are scattered, and Earth suddenly feels very far away.

Fortunately, the comet has left behind a trail of debris, forming an unexpected path through space. With no other choice, you begin bouncing from one piece of debris to another, travelling between asteroids and planets in an attempt to reach your spaceship.

Along the way, you must recover its missing components and collect valuable Space Gem Shards to restore its power.

But space holds more than just rocks and empty darkness. Mysterious and hostile creatures inhabit the depths of the universe, posing another threat to your already difficult journey.

Will you manage to return to Earth and tell your story?

Probably not.

But determination is what matters.

Gameplay

Lost in Orbit revolves around navigating a low-gravity environment and using the surrounding space debris to progress through the game.

Core Mechanics
Low-Gravity Movement: Experience movement in space, where gravity and momentum influence how you navigate the environment.
Debris Platforming: Bounce between floating rocks, asteroids, and planets to make your way across space.
Space Exploration: Travel through different areas of the universe while searching for a path back to your spaceship.
Collectibles: Gather Space Gem Shards scattered throughout the environment.
Spaceship Restoration: Recover missing spaceship components and collect resources needed to power your ship.
Enemies: Encounter mysterious creatures lurking throughout the depths of space.
Survival: Navigate environmental challenges and make progress before time runs out.
How It Works

The game is designed around the relationship between movement, gravity, and the environment.

1. Movement and Physics

Unlike traditional platformers, Lost in Orbit takes place in a low-gravity environment. Movement relies on momentum and controlled interactions with the surrounding objects.

Players must adapt to the physics of space to travel effectively and avoid losing control of their movement.

2. Platforming Through Space

Floating debris serves as the main platforming environment. Players use rocks and other celestial objects as stepping stones, bouncing between them to reach new areas.

This creates a platforming experience where positioning, timing, and movement control are essential.

3. Collecting Resources

Space Gem Shards are scattered throughout the environment. Collecting them contributes towards restoring the power of the damaged spaceship.

Players must explore their surroundings and collect the resources needed to complete their journey.

4. Progression and Survival

As players travel further through space, they encounter new obstacles and hostile creatures. Progress requires balancing exploration, resource collection, and survival while working towards reaching the spaceship.

Development

Lost in Orbit was developed as a collaborative game development project using the Godot Engine.

The development process involved designing and implementing the game's mechanics, creating its gameplay environment, and bringing together the different elements needed to deliver a playable platforming experience.

Game Engine

Godot Engine

Godot provides the foundation for the game's development, including its scene system, physics, rendering, and scripting capabilities.

Programming

GDScript

GDScript is used to implement the game's logic and interactions, including player movement, gameplay mechanics, and interactions with the environment.

Physics and Gameplay Systems

The physics system plays an important role in the overall experience. Particular attention is given to low-gravity movement and momentum-based platforming to make navigating space feel different from conventional platformers.

Level and Environment Design

The environment is built around floating celestial objects and scattered debris. These objects form the pathways players use to navigate through space and create the foundation for the game's platforming challenges.
